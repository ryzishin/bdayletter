A LITTLE BIRTHDAY LETTER — a handmade lavender scrapbook for Palangging
=========================================================================

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
Just double-click index.html — it works straight from the folder,
no installs, no internet needed (the cute fonts load online, with
friendly fallbacks offline).

To put it online, drop the whole folder into any static host
(Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, or whatever you like).

WHAT'S INSIDE
-------------
index.html   the scrapbook page and your letter
style.css    the lavender design, doodles and animations
script.js    the letter-opening magic and scroll reveals
assets/      photo-1 to photo-4 (the four real photos, in
             cream polaroid frames with washi tape)

THE FOUR PHOTOS
---------------
photo-1  childhood, beside the refrigerator  (shown with photo-2)
photo-2  childhood, eating a snack           (shown with photo-1)
photo-3  the funny close-up face             (right after HAHAHAHA)
photo-4  the film-strip double portrait      (near the ending)

Want different pictures? Just replace those files in assets/
keeping the same names. Photos appear with no captions — they are
pure scrapbook decoration, as requested.

EASY EDITS
----------
* Her name in the greeting: search index.html for "PALANGGING!"
* The message: it is kept word for word, including the Bisaya and
  the two purple hearts. Edit the <p class="letter-p"> blocks.
* The sign-off "— your palangga" sits just under the last line.
* Colors: they all live at the top of style.css as variables.

MADE WITH
---------
HTML + CSS + vanilla JavaScript. Exactly three files, no
frameworks, no build tools. Decorations are hand-drawn inline SVG
(flowers, smileys, stars, hearts, washi tape, and the little
arrow that draws itself).
